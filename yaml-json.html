Frequently asked questions

What YAML features are supported?

Nested mappings and lists (block and flow style), strings, numbers, booleans, null, and comments. Advanced features like anchors, aliases, multi-document files, and block scalars (| or >) are not supported.

Why did my YAML fail to convert?

Usually inconsistent indentation, tabs instead of spaces, or an unsupported feature like anchors/aliases or block scalars. Try simplifying the file to a plain mapping/list structure.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The conversion happens entirely in your browser.
