Frequently asked questions

When do I need to URL-encode text?

Whenever a value (like a search query or parameter) might contain spaces, symbols, or non-ASCII characters and needs to be placed inside a URL, such as a query string.

What's the difference between encodeURIComponent and encodeURI?

encodeURIComponent (used here) escapes characters like & and = so it's safe for a single query parameter value. encodeURI leaves those characters alone since it's meant for encoding a whole URL.

Is my input sent to a server?

No. Encoding and decoding both happen locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
