FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which regex flavor does this use?

This uses JavaScript's native RegExp engine (ECMAScript regex), the same one used in browsers and Node.js.

What do the g, i, m, s flags mean?

g finds all matches instead of just the first. i makes matching case-insensitive. m makes ^ and $ match the start/end of each line. s makes . also match newlines.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Matching happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript's built-in regex engine.
