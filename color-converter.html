FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What color formats does this tool accept?

You can paste a HEX code (#fff or #ffffff), an rgb()/rgba() value, or an hsl()/hsla() value. The tool automatically detects the format and converts it to the other two.

What's the difference between HSL and RGB?

RGB defines a color by its red, green, and blue light components. HSL defines it by hue (the color itself), saturation (intensity), and lightness — which many designers find more intuitive for adjusting a color.

Is my input sent to a server?

No. All color parsing and conversion happens locally in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
