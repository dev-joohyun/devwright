FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is MD5 safe to use?

No. MD5 is considered cryptographically broken and should not be used for security purposes like password storage. It's included here for checksums and compatibility with legacy systems only.

Which hash should I use for passwords?

None of these on their own. Passwords should be hashed with a dedicated, slow algorithm designed for that purpose (like bcrypt, scrypt, or Argon2), not a general-purpose hash like SHA-256.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Every hash is computed locally in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
