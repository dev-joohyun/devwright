FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a Unix timestamp in seconds or milliseconds?

The classic Unix timestamp is in seconds since January 1, 1970 UTC. Many APIs and JavaScript's Date.now() use milliseconds instead. This tool auto-detects which one you pasted based on its length.

Does this show local time or UTC?

Both. The result shows your browser's local time as well as the UTC time and the ISO 8601 string.

Is my input sent to a server?

No. The conversion happens entirely in your browser using standard JavaScript Date APIs.
